# Gallery

Eleven larger models, released under CC0: a castle, a lighthouse, a pagoda,
a sailing ship, a windmill, an amphitheatre, an Eiffel-style tower, a step
pyramid, a Greek temple, a triumphal arch, and Notre-Dame de Paris, the
largest, with 1392 planks in 29 steps. Each one stands at every placement in the
balance check and in a drop test. Open one in the player to replay it step by
step, plank by plank.

## Download the models

The source of every model is a plain `.plank.md` file. They are in the
[`gallery/`](https://github.com/plankmd/plankmd/tree/main/gallery) and
[`samples/`](https://github.com/plankmd/plankmd/tree/main/samples) folders of
the repository, and in the npm package.

To open your own model, drop it on the player, or link to it:
`https://plankmd.org/player/?src=<url of the .plank.md file>`. The file must
be served with CORS allowed, as raw files on GitHub are.

## Share your own models

The gallery is a fixed selection. Your own models live in your own
repository, under the license you choose: put the `.plank.md` files in a
public GitHub repository and share
`https://plankmd.org/p?repo=<your repository>`. It opens them as a gallery
and in the player, straight from GitHub. See [Use it](/use#share-your-models-from-github).
